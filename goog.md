🏢 Company Overview
-------------------

Alphabet Inc. is a multinational technology conglomerate and the parent company
of Google. The company operates through Google Services (Search, YouTube, Gmail,
Maps, Play Store), Google Cloud (enterprise cloud computing), and Other Bets
(autonomous vehicles via Waymo, life sciences, smart city initiatives). As one
of the world's most valuable companies, Alphabet dominates digital advertising
and is a leading force in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and emerging
technologies with over 2 billion users across its core products.
